📝 Sign-up & usage guide

  1. Click 🔑 Open console (top right) to open the console panel, then click 「Sign up」 in the panel to create an account in a new tab: username + email + password (≥8 chars), no invite code needed.
  2. After signing up, log back in to the panel to enter the BYOK console.
  3. In the radar list below, click «Apply for key» to get a free KEY at the vendor (limits per vendor are shown in the list).
  4. Back in the panel, click «+ Add BYOK channel», fill in vendor name, BASE URL, and your KEY → save.
  5. Call via the unified gateway entry; traffic auto-routes to your BYOK channel. The KEY stays local only.
